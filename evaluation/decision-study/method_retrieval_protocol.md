# Does a selected passage retrieve papers sharing its method?

Fixed before scoring the three experiments. No training or parameter selection on these labels. The reader policy and its training corpus stay frozen. Primary endpoint is the change in SciERC visible-name Hit@3 for fusion against the Reader. Other endpoints are diagnostic. No production switch follows from a proxy improvement.

* SciERC: use the first contiguous, source-verifiable Method annotation in each abstract whose case- and punctuation-normalized name occurs as an annotated Method in another abstract. Use its entire source sentence as the query and all other abstracts as candidates. A positive shares the annotated name. Repeat with every literal occurrence of the selected surface in that sentence masked. Record eligible documents, distinct names, span failures, candidate counts and counts by name. This deliberately samples recurring techniques, including generic terms.
* ACL-ARC: use official test Uses citation contexts with a known cited title and removable target citation marker. Index distinct cited titles from train/dev/test (catalog only). Remove the citing title and targets known to postdate the citing paper. Query with the marker-stripped sentence plus the citing title. A positive is the actual cited title. This evaluates known-item recovery over titles and includes resources as well as techniques.
* CSFCube: reuse the nine method queries of the already inspected fold1 test, the saved Reader scores, selected sentence, candidate pools and graded judgments. Positive means grade at least 2. This is a diagnostic on the original task.
* Arms: frozen lexical–semantic Reader, BM25 unigram, BM25 unigram plus adjacent bigram (k1 1.2, b 0.75, unigram document length, each query feature once), equal reciprocal rank fusion of Reader and bigram BM25 (k=60, average tied ranks), then a frozen MS MARCO cross-encoder reranking the fusion top 20. Ranks outside the shortlist are preserved. Candidate IDs break final ties. The cross-encoder receives the same textual query and candidate. No gold name, citations, grades or intent are ranking features.
* Report Hit@3, useful slots at 3, graded nDCG@10, first useful rank and Hit@20 (shortlist ceiling). Estimate paired differences by 10,000 cluster bootstrap replicates, clustered by normalized technique, citing paper and seed query respectively. Resample clusters with replacement, retaining their queries and query-weighting within each replicate. The visible/masked comparison measures sensitivity to the supplied term, not conceptual equivalence.

Pre-score source correction: all 1,941 ACL-ARC records carry the same citing title across many distinct IDs. Discard that field entirely. Use the citation sentence alone, remove self by source/target ID (including alternate IDs attached to the same candidate title) and use the minimum known year of a deduplicated title. Treat the source data as supplied, with catalog corruption a limitation.

Catalog identity correction based on metadata audit: 23 target IDs have multiple raw titles, including two Penn Treebank titles. Merge the connected components of target IDs and normalized titles (a match on either joins records). Index the shortest title per component, breaking ties lexically. Choose no names using scores or relevance labels. Filter dates across the component and remove the source ID from eligible results. Canonical candidate IDs break score ties. The shortlist reranker breaks equal logits by incoming fusion rank.
